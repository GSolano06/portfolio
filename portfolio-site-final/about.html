About

I'm an electrical engineering student at the University of Pittsburgh's Swanson School of Engineering.

I enjoy exploring hardware, logic, and sensor work. So far most of my projects have been hardware, from digital logic to audio and sensing.

When I build, I work in stages and check each stage before adding the next. On the multiplier, that meant checking against the truth table, and its LED outputs match on all 16 input combinations.
