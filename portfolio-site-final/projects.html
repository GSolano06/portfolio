Earlier projects

  • Oct 2024 to May 2025

    Auto-EQ, high school capstone

    A gain controller that attenuates audio output in proportion to ambient sound level, using an SPI digital potentiometer as a voltage divider with 256 steps. An exponential moving-average filter in embedded C++ smooths the gain changes. I could not integrate a live microphone, so I validated the control logic against simulated sound input.

  • Dec 2022 to May 2023

    Concussion Sensing Device

    A head-impact detector built with teammates in my Career Technical Engineering program, using an Arduino and an accelerometer to show severity on LEDs from g-force thresholds. I tested it with controlled drop heights, and the LED tier matched the expected severity in 90 of 100 trials.
